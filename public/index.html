How to play

Settings

Joystick on: touch the left half of the screen to move, touch the right half to aim (hold to draw the bow, let go to fire), and use the DASH button. Off: touch and drag to steer toward your finger, hold to draw.

Account

An account keeps your points and skins safe and lets you play on any device. Your guest points and skins move into a new account when you create it.
